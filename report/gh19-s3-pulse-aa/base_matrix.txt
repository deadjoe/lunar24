-- STATIC residual matrix (route: analytic Fourier) --
   res_db = 总诊断残差, dB vs the reference's own RMS. floor_db / harm_db are the two shares
   of the residual in LINEAR power: floor_db = 10*log10(floor/total), harm_db likewise, with
   floor + harm = total. The two LINEAR shares therefore sum to 1; the two dB NUMBERS DO NOT
   sum to 0 (an earlier revision of this header claimed they did -- corrected). The partition
   is by FREQUENCY NEIGHBOURHOOD (k = round(f/f0)), not by alias identity: a folded image
   that lands on a legitimate harmonic is counted as harmonic, so this split cannot identify
   aliases, only bound how much of the residual sits away from the harmonic comb. floor_db
   down is necessary but NOT sufficient evidence that aliasing fell.
   res_1k5k_db / res_effbd_db are the BAND-LIMITED OUTPUT RESIDUALS the contract asks for:
   10*log10(band power of (x - A*r) / band power of (A*r)), one declared denominator, Hann
   window, power-normalized by sum(w^2), and res_split's DC/Nyquist half-weight. They are NOT
   the decimator's passband flatness -- that is a precondition, asserted in --self-check (d).
   The effective band here is the analytic reference's own limit: the series is truncated at
